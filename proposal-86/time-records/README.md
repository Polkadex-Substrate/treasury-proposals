# Polkadex core team time records, November 2024 to October 2026

Supporting record for **Polkadex treasury proposal 86** (core team funding, 22 October 2026). This is a readable, public version of the time-record workbook cited in the proposal. Nothing is summarised away: every task log entry appears in the [monthly files](#monthly-detail) as logged, and the full data is in [`data/`](data/). Personal names are replaced with pseudonyms or roles, and access details are redacted; see [Redactions](#redactions).

## How to check this record

The proposal cites the original workbook by its SHA-256 hash, `676ad078be4c06fb5f8613821de68a8ed9dc0e202c6bd929aecafdf2256cb719`. The original is not published here because it contains the unredacted names and access details; it is available to the Council on request, as stated in the proposal, and the files in this folder can be regenerated from it and compared.

The files in this folder have their own hashes, listed in `SHA256SUMS`. To check the figures, open `data/task-log.csv` and sum the hours column.

Notes on the source:

- The workbook's Est. Hours, Priority, Invoice Month and approval columns are empty; no estimates or approvals were recorded. They are kept in `data/task-log.csv`.
- The workbook's Summary sheet header reads "14/10/2024 – 14/10/2026": that is the date range of the tracker export. The earliest engineer entry is 2 June 2025 and core team time starts 1 November 2024, so nothing falls before 1 November 2024.
- The Task ID column is empty in the source. Each entry is identified here by its source row number.
- Apart from the redactions above, descriptions are reproduced as logged, including typos and formatting lost in the export (for example missing spaces around code names).
- The workbook's Summary sheet (totals by category, by person and by week) is derived from the Task Log and Core Team sheets; the tables above are recalculated from the same rows and agree with it.
